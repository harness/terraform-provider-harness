```release-note:enhancement
resource/harness_platform_gitops_app_project_mapping: Added `block_if_referenced` (Boolean, Optional, Default: `true`) to opt in to a safety check on delete — if any Applications, Clusters, Repositories, or ApplicationSets still reference the mapping's Argo project, the delete fails instead of cascading. Set to `false` to keep the previous cascade-delete-everything behavior. Also applies when the resource is replaced due to a change in `agent_id` or `argo_project_name`.
```
